The WXDU Record Fair is back! 14 vendor tables will be set up in the **Ruby Lounge** (inside the Rubenstein Arts Center) with records, CDs, cassettes and more to buy.

Admission is **FREE**! Free parking is available for all record fair attendees in the Campus Drive Lot, across the street from the Rubenstein Arts Center (corner of Anderson St. & Campus Dr).

The event is **CASH ONLY**. Checks are also fine, but electronic payments are not allowed. Swing by the ATM on the way there.

Hope to see you there!
